Help & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our AI Career Coach platform.

How does the ATS Resume Analyzer evaluate my resume?
Our engine scans your PDF or DOCX file using automated text extraction, parsing technical skills, work experience keywords, section headings, and layout formatting. It compares these attributes against actual job description screeners used by tech recruiters.
How do the AI Mock Interviews work?
The mock interview simulator prompts candidate questions based on your chosen target role. You can respond via voice audio or text typing. The AI analyzes technical correctness, speech pace, and filler words, providing an instant score and feedback report.
How are the 6-Week Career Roadmaps generated?
Based on your identified skill gaps, our platform constructs a 6-week curriculum with weekly milestones, practice projects, and curated learning links specific to software engineering, data science, or full-stack roles.
Is AI Career Coach suitable for campus placements?
Yes! AI Career Coach is specifically designed for college students preparing for campus recruitment drives, off-campus placements, and tech internships.
Can I start for free without a credit card?
Yes, our Free Starter plan is 100% free with no credit card required. You can test your ATS resume score and explore company preparation guides immediately.
